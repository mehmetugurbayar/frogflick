Frog Flick – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Frog Flick ("the game") is a casual game published by Mehmet Uğur Bayar. This policy explains what information the game uses.

What we collect

The game has no accounts and we do not collect, store or sell any personal information on our own servers. Your best score and settings (music, sound, haptics, language, frog colour) are saved only on your device.

Advertising

The game shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use data such as your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking), IP address, device and usage information, and ad interactions to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Where required (for example in the EEA and the UK) you will first see a consent form from Google. On iOS you can allow or deny tracking in the prompt shown when you first open the game, and change it any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. You can also limit ad personalisation in your device settings. More information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
